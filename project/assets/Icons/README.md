# Icons

Two inks in every file: `pink` (#fb49b6) for the primary form and `cyan` (#48e1fd) for the detail, 2px round strokes on a 48-unit grid inside a 56-unit box, with the glow baked in. Dark grounds only; in code, use `Icon`.

- `ai-systems.svg`: AI Systems & Automation (half brain, half circuit).
- `business-growth.svg`: Business Growth (rising bars under a trend arrow).
- `web-app-development.svg`: Web & App Development (angle brackets and slash).
- `experimental-marketing.svg`: Experimental Marketing (megaphone).
- `brand-strategy.svg`: Brand Strategy (crosshair target).
- `3d-virtualization.svg`: 3D Virtualization & Events (isometric cube).
- `sales-leadership.svg`: Sales Leadership (a lead figure with two others).
- `innovation-mentorship.svg`: Innovation & Mentorship (lit bulb).
