# Halo

The neon ring behind the subject, violet at the top turning blue on the right and pink on the left, with a soft bloom.

**You provide** the subject as children, usually a portrait `<img>` cropped to head and shoulders; it is clipped to a circle inside the ring. With `clip={false}` a cut-out portrait stands in front of the ring instead, as on the key visual. `size` is the diameter (default 280px).

Use one halo per composition, behind the main subject, and light portraits to match: a pink rim from the left, blue from the right. Don't put text or icons in the ring or stack rings.
