# AskAnswer (Pink asks, cyan answers)

Voice and social format that pairs with Chaos Gap. Approved by Andries on 2026-10-19.

## Turn order

1. **Ask (pink):** a real client question in the client's words, or one of the approved Concierge starter questions:
   - "I need a website. What does it cost?"
   - "Can you automate my WhatsApp enquiries?"
   - "I need a logo and brand. Where do I start?"
   - "Book a call with Andries"
2. **Decision (Chaos Gap):** recommended offer + list price + one next step.
3. **Answer (cyan):** short reply that includes the list price (or points at the priced offer in the gap) and names one next step. First person ("I"), no "we", no emoji, no exclamation marks.

## Rules

- AN3S never speaks first on AskAnswer surfaces.
- Every Answer must carry an official list price from the approved price sheet. Quote-only work uses the existing "Scoped with you" treatment and, where it applies, a priced first step (Discovery Sprint, etc.).
- Do not invent results, turnaround times, client names or testimonials.
- Display question length on social may run to about 12 words in `pink-glow`; answers stay sentence case in `heading` / `body` with priced names in `cyan-glow`.
- Default social size: 1080 × 1350 (see size tokens). Other sizes must keep the same turn order.
