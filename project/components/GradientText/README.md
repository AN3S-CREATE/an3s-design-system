# GradientText

One uppercase tagline line filled with `gradient-glow`: pink on the left, violet in the middle, cyan on the right.

It frames a composition: the top line ("Fueling growth at the intersection of") and the foot line ("Strategy, Chaos, and Experimental Tech"). **You provide** the words as children in normal case; they render uppercase in the `tagline` style (20px, 600, 0.32em tracking). `align` is `center` by default, which also corrects for the trailing tracking.

Use at most two per composition, one at the top and one at the foot. Don't use it for headings or anything that wraps, and never reverse the gradient: light travels pink to cyan.
